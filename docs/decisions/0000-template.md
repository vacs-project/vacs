# NNNN: Title

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD, amended YYYY-MM-DD (what changed), implemented YYYY-MM-DD
- Source: recording directory and timestamp, or other origin of the decision

## Context

What situation forced a decision. Keep it to the facts a reader needs in order to
understand why doing nothing was not an option.

## Decision

What we decided, in present tense. One decision per record. Name the alternatives
only in the next section.

## Why (and what we rejected)

The reasoning, and every alternative that was seriously considered together with the
reason it lost. This section is the point of the record. If the rationale for a part
of the decision was not recorded, say so instead of inventing one.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what work the decision commits us to.
Once implemented, name the code that carries the decision (paths and key symbols) so
the record can be checked against reality.
